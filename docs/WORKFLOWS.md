# Operator walkthrough

## Full QR → manual addition → kitchen → split payment → closing

Use separate browser profiles/private windows for distinct staff identities; this demo revokes prior token versions when a user refreshes or signs out. Use different demo users for simultaneous roles.

1. Run setup, API and web. Sign in as `owner@demo.test`.
2. Under **Accounts**, create a cash drawer with opening balance 0 and a bank account, or use the empty seeded accounts.
3. In a separate browser sign in as `cashier@demo.test`; open **Cashier shifts**, choose the empty drawer, opening count 0. Cash payment requires a currently open drawer assigned to its receiving user.
4. As owner open **Tables & QR**, create an available test table, then **New QR**. Download its SVG or open the customer menu. Generating a new QR invalidates the previous one. For real phone scanning, set `PUBLIC_WEB_URL` to an HTTPS address reachable by the phone and restart the API; a laptop-local loopback URL is not reachable from the phone.
5. On the customer page choose **Continue**. Pick Garlic Naan, quantity 2, then **Place order**. The accepted order is NEW, with **Self-order (QR)** attribution. A new unrelated customer browser cannot see it even with the same table QR.
6. Sign in as `waiter@demo.test` in another browser, open **New order**, select the same table, add one regular Fresh Lime. The manual round shares the current open table session and preserves the waiter name/staff ID. The estimated combined total for this no-tax demo is PKR 420.00.
7. As owner/manager **Orders → Confirm** the QR round. Kitchen staff see only assigned kitchen stations; bar staff see their assigned bar station. Under **Kitchen & bar**, move each item to preparing, then ready. As waiter move dine-in items to served. Takeaway moves from ready directly to completed.
8. As owner **Billing → Generate bill**, choose the table session. The bill combines its rounds, lists the unique staff taker plus Self-order (QR), and records the owner as generator. Finalization is immutable. Do not add new rounds after billing: settle and start a new session.
9. As cashier receive a **cash payment of 20000 minor units** (PKR 200.00); if tendered is 25000, change is PKR 50.00. Then record the remaining **22000** into the bank account with a transaction reference. Non-cash is pending until a permitted verifier explicitly checks it.
10. As owner/manager verify the bank payment in Billing or Accounts. The bill settles and session closes; table moves to cleaning. Staff mark it available after turnover. Printing never creates payment.
11. Queue the receipt and open **Print queue → Dispatch**. Choose a 58/80 mm destination. Check preview, printer and paper before printing. Reprints require a reason and create a separate audited job.
12. Cashier closes the shift with physical count **20000**. Expected cash is **20000**, variance 0, assuming no other movements used that test drawer. The bank collection does not enter expected drawer cash. Manager can review the closing.

The real PostgreSQL integration suite automates these domain operations, including two simultaneous attempts to bill the same items and idempotent payment retry. Browser smoke covers navigation and real QR submission/own-order tracking; it does not assert physical printer output.

## Menu and branch isolation

Owners create tenant categories/products under **Menu & setup**. A product is shared across the tenant, but its branch price, availability and station are configured under **Branch menu**. Managers cannot edit tenant-wide product definitions; they can configure only assigned branch overrides. Keep at least one valid preparation station assigned for every orderable branch product. Deactivate existing products instead of deleting history.

Configure variants and modifier groups in the product editor. Variant IDs and option IDs are stable internal identifiers generated by the form. Selection limits are checked again by the server. Upload a menu image, then use its returned image reference in the product editor. No demo stock photography is included, so no third-party image attribution is needed.

## PIN security and QR lifecycle

When **Require current table-session PIN** is enabled in Settings, staff open a table session with a random PIN before customers join. Share the current PIN privately. It is not encoded in the permanent QR. A QR alone does not prove physical presence. Staff confirmation is always required for submitted QR orders, with PIN enforcement adding a second check.

A session's PIN is set when it is first opened; opening an already active session does not reset it. Close/settle before starting the next session. QR rotation invalidates the printed link, but an already issued customer capability can still read its own prior orders until expiry; it cannot submit into a closed session.

## Purchases, expenses and returns

1. In Menu & setup create a supplier and a purchase catalogue item with its unit.
2. In Purchases & expenses record a supplier invoice with quantities and unit costs in minor units. Recognition records a cost and payable; it does not move cash.
3. Pay part or all from a branch financial account. For manual non-cash allocations, use Accounts to verify or reject the reference. Payment is counted once by the ledger.
4. Record a purchase return with its value/reason. If this creates a paid supplier credit, the Supplier refund action records the actual bounded money returned.
5. Record rent/utilities as expenses with their own payment allocations. Do not record a supplier purchase again as an operating expense. Cash-out notes are optional.

## Staff and platform

Owner staff forms support granular permission combinations, multiple branch assignments and preparation station assignments. Staff IDs cannot change after creation. Existing profiles can be disabled; tokens are version-revoked when access changes. Basic schedule entries live in Menu & setup → Staff schedule.

Run the separate platform service for tenant provisioning, trial/active/suspended/expired subscription control and storage allowance API. Platform support access requires a reason and returns a bounded, read-only tenant operational projection with an immutable audit entry; it never impersonates tenant staff. Staff tokens fail at the platform service and platform tokens fail at the tenant API.

## Advanced operations available through REST

Swagger and API.md document audited order-taker reassignment, charge waivers, drawer handover, discretionary cash reversals, password changes, and paginated history. The core staff screens cover daily ordering and financial work; some advanced commands are API-first rather than having dedicated workflow screens. Consult API.md for exact payloads and permissions.

## Bill history and discounts

In **Billing**, every bill has a **History** button showing its read-only activity timeline. Review creation and discounts, individual payment actions with the actual receiver/verifier, settlement, refunds, void reasons and receipt queue/dispatch/reprints. The history is scoped to that bill's tenant/branch and paginates longer timelines.

When generating a bill, permitted staff can choose **Fixed amount** or **Percentage**, enter the normal currency amount/percent and an optional note. The server validates the value and calculates the final charge breakdown. A cashier without discount permission cannot apply a discount by modifying the request. Finalized bill totals are still immutable; use controlled refund/void adjustments for later corrections.
